Overview

Klondike solitaire is played with a regular deck of cards dealt to seven piles increasing in number from one to seven with only the top cards facing up.  The total number of cards dealt is 28 leaving 24 cards in the stock. The object of the game is to move all of the cards to the foundations.  There is one foundation for each suit and they must be placed in ascending suit sequence from Ace to King.  Only the top exposed card of the tableau and discard piles may be moved to the foundations one card at a time.  You can move a card to the foundation by double tapping it or tap-dragging it to the foundation.

Playing

To play, build on the exposed cards of the tableau piles down in value.  You may begin play by either alternating colored suits or building the piles down in the same suit.  Building down in the same suit is harder.  Whichever method you begin your game with will hold for the remainder of the game.  Whenever the exposed cards of a pile are cleared, the next card in the pile flips up and is available for play.  If a pile is completely cleared, it can then only be played on with a King.  Cards can be moved one at a time or by multiple selection between the tableau piles.  To play from the discard pile, tap the stock to expose the next three cards.  The top most exposed card in the discard pile is available for play on the foundations or tableau piles.  After all cards are dealt to the discard pile, tap the stock area to replenish the stock.

Game Options

Game Options include the ability to have 1 or 3 cards drawn from the stock, have the cards being moved displayed normally or with inverted colors, and select from 5 different card back styles ( or '?' to have the style randomly selected at the beginning of each game ).  These options are set by selecting 'Options : Customize' from the menu.

Another game option is to play the tableau piles down with the same suit rather than alternating colored suits.  Playing down with the same suit is a harder way to play.  The game must be completed the way it is started.
