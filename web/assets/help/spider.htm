Overview

Spider solitaire is played with 2 decks of cards dealt into 10 piles.  The first 4 piles have 5 cards down and the last 6 have 4 down.  Each pile is then dealt 1 card face up making a total of 54 cards dealt.  The remaining 50 cards are left in the stock.

The object of the game is to get 13 cards of a suit on top of a pile from King down to Ace.  Each time a suit is assembled, it will be removed from the pile.  The game is won if you get all 8 suits assembled.  The tableau piles can be built down without regard to suit.  However, only same suit sequences can be moved (optionally, mixed suits can be moved).  A good strategy is to first make all of your same suit moves before mixing suits and to free up the face down cards from the piles as soon as possible.  When a pile is cleared, you must occupy that space with a card before dealing from the stock.  When no more moves are possible, tap the stock to deal 10 more cards face up.   Play continues until all cards are dealt and all possible moves are made.

Playing

To play the game, tap and drag cards from pile to pile, building down in the same suit from King to Ace.  When a suit is fully assembled ( King to Ace ), those cards will be removed from the game.  The object of the game is to remove all of the cards from the tableau piles by assembling all 8 sets of cards.

A good strategy is to first make all of your same suit moves before mixing suits and to free up the face down cards from the piles as soon as possible.  When a pile is cleared, you must occupy that space with a card of any value before dealing from the stock.  When no more moves are possible, tap the stock to deal 10 more cards face up.  Play continues until all cards are dealt and all possible moves are made.

Game Options

From the menu, select 'Options : Customize' to set the game's difficulty and appearance.

Select any of the 5 card styles or the '?' card to have the card style randomly selected before each game.  The 'Move Selected Cards Style' is how the cards look when being moved and the 'Valid Move Cards Style' is how the cards will look when moved over a pile which the cards can be moved to.  Beginners might want to select the 'Inverted Colors' move selected cards style and 'Normal' valid move cards style.  With these settings, the selected cards will change from inverted to normal when moved over a pile which they can be moved to.

Difficulty
   1 Suit is easiest -- means that only 1 suit will be used in the game;
   2 Suits is medium -- means that only 2 suits will be used;
   4 Suits is most difficult -- means that all 4 suits will be used.

You may also optionally allow mixed suit cards to be moved by checking the box, 'Allow Mixed Suit Moves'.   When checked, mixed suit cards built down in sequence can be moved.  This is an easier way to play the game.

The difficulty settings take effect when you start a new game.
