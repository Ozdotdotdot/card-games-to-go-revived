Overview

War is a simple game of chance in which a regular 52 card deck is divided in half between 2 players.  Each player turns up a card and the player with the highest value card takes both face up cards and places them on the bottom of their deck.  When both cards are the same value, this is WAR!  The players then place 3 more cards face down and the 4th up.  The player with the highest face up card takes all 10.  If the face up cards are the same value ( draw ), each player places 1 card down and another up.  This play continues until the face up cards do not have the same value.  The player with the highest value card takes all of the cards placed during the war.

Depending on the options set, play continues until either all cards are won or a preset number of wars have been won.

Other options include the number of cards placed down during a war and draw.  These options can be set from the 'Options : Customize' dialog.

 

Playing

After the computer turns up the top card from its deck, tap your deck to turn up your top card.  The player with the highest value card takes the hand and the cards are placed on the bottom of the winner's deck.  If the top cards from each deck have the same value, this is WAR!  The computer will then turn 3 cards face down and the 4th up.  You will then need to tap your deck to deal 3 of your cards down and the 4th up.  The hand with the highest face up card wins all 10 cards, which are placed on the bottom of the winner's deck.  If the war results in a draw, the computer will then place one more card down and a second card up.  You will then need to tap your deck twice to deal one card down and the second card up.  This play continues until one hand is a winner.

Play continues until one player wins all of the cards or 3 wars.  From the menu, select 'Options : Customize' to define how many wars need to be won or if all cards need to be won to win the game and how many cards are dealt down during war and draw.

To change the characters, tap within the character's rectangle.

To change the card style, tap the computer's deck.
